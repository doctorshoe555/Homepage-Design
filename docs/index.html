From brief to delivery in six steps

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
