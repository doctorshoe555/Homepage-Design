Dynamic outboard tow cable for a submarine

A towed cable that is paid out, recovered and bent over sheaves again and again, while carrying power and data under pressure.

Sector
Naval defence
Cable type
Dynamic hybrid tow cable
Scope
Design, qualification, production

The challenge

The customer needed an outboard tow cable that could be deployed and recovered from a submarine many times without losing signal integrity. The cable had to resist hydrostatic pressure, high tensile load and continuous bending over the handling system.

Our approach

Our engineers combined conductors, signal elements and a strength member in one compact construction, chose sheath materials for seawater and abrasion, and validated the design with the customer before series production.

The result

A cable built for the exact handling system and mission profile, supplied in the lengths the programme needed.
