Umbilical for the ELODIE burial machine

An umbilical that powers and controls a subsea burial machine working on the seabed.

Sector
Subsea and defence
Cable type
Subsea umbilical
Scope
Custom design and supply

The challenge

A burial machine working on the seabed needs power, control and data from the surface vessel through one cable that is dragged, lifted and exposed to abrasion and seawater for long periods.

Our approach

We designed an umbilical that brings the required electrical and signal functions together with the mechanical strength for lifting and towing, and matched it to the machine's handling equipment.

The result

One umbilical for power, control and data, sized for the machine and the vessel it operates from.
