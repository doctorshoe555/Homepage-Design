The LAPP Group's special cable specialist

Since 2003, LAPP Muller in Grimaud has been the technology centre of the LAPP Group for cables that do not exist in any catalogue.

Who we are

Our team in the Var designs and manufactures fully customised cables for industries where conditions are extreme and failure is expensive: defence, nuclear, subsea, energy, robotics and medical.

We build in any length and size, keep lead times short, and aim to solve problems before they arise. That mission has not changed since the first cable left Grimaud.

Part of the LAPP Group

The family-owned LAPP Group, headquartered in Stuttgart, is a leading supplier of integrated solutions in cable and connection technology.

For our customers, that means the engineering focus of a specialist with the purchasing power, global presence and stability of a group. Standard products come from the LAPP catalogue; LAPP Muller takes over where the catalogue ends.

Visit lappgroup.com

Thinking innovatively, acting sustainably

Long service life is the most sustainable property a cable can have. We design for durability, choose halogen-free materials where possible, and support the electrification of ports and airports with our shore power and 400 Hz ranges.

Group-wide sustainability goals and reports are published by the LAPP Group.
