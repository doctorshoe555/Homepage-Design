Industries we serve

Eight fields, one approach: we start from the conditions your cable will face and design backwards from there.

  • Defence and naval

    Tow cables, umbilicals and power cables for submarines, surface ships and subsea systems.

  • Electrification

    Shore power cables for ports and 400 Hz ground power cables for aircraft.

  • Nuclear

    Radiation-resistant, halogen-free cables for power plants, reprocessing and research.

  • Marine and subsea

    ROV tethers, umbilicals, floating cables and sonar cables for the underwater world.

  • Oil and gas

    Offshore umbilicals and onshore instrumentation, control and power cables.

  • Robotics and automation

    Extra-flexible torsion and drag-chain cables for robots and moving machines.

  • Medical

    Cables for imaging, life support, therapy and surgical robots.

  • Mountain, handling and inspection

    Reeling, flat, mountain, fibre and inspection cables for extreme duty.

Not on the list?

Most of our projects begin with a question nobody has asked before. If your cable has to survive something unusual, we want to hear about it.

What helps us answer fast

  • Where the cable is installed and how it moves
  • Electrical, optical or fluid functions it carries
  • Temperature, media and standards to meet
  • Length, quantity and target date
