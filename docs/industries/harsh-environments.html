Mountain, handling and inspection cables

For cables under high mechanical stress, we build strong designs that resist abrasion and extreme climatic and chemical conditions, from ski lifts to pipe inspection.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Mountain cables for lifts and snow equipment
  • Reeling and flat cables for handling equipment
  • Fibre optic cables for harsh routes
  • Inspection cables for cameras and crawlers

What you get

  • High tensile strength with aramid or steel reinforcement
  • Abrasion, cold and chemical resistant sheaths
  • Hybrid copper and fibre in one cable
  • Any length, including short runs

How a mountain, handling and inspection project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study
