Robotics and automation cables

Robot arms and drag chains move millions of times. Our extra-flexible cables are built with class 6 conductors and special polymers to keep up.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Bending and torsion cables for industrial robots
  • Drag-chain power and control cables
  • Miniaturised cables for compact automation
  • Screened signal cables for motion control

What you get

  • Extra-flexible class 6 conductors to IEC 60228 and NF C 32-013
  • Tinned copper braid screens with high optical coverage
  • Miniaturised designs in special polymers
  • Screened or unscreened, to your motion profile

How a robotics and automation project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.
