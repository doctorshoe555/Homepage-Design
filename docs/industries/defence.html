Defence and naval cables

Cables for naval platforms that have to survive pressure, repeated bending over sheaves and decades in service, built to your specification and qualified with you.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Dynamic outboard tow cables for submarines
  • Umbilicals for subsea burial and mine countermeasure systems
  • Ship power, control and instrumentation cables
  • Sonar and hydrophone cables
  • Cable assemblies with connectors and terminations

What you get

  • Hybrid designs that combine power, signal, fibre and strength members in one cable
  • Small series and one-off lengths for qualification and prototypes
  • Programmes handled under confidentiality agreements
  • Certification organised with the certifying body your programme requires
Design envelope
ConstructionHybrid: electrical, optical, pneumatic, hydraulic, strength members
MechanicalDynamic bending, torsion, high tensile load, crush resistance
EnvironmentSeawater, hydrostatic pressure, UV, hydrocarbons
LengthsFrom a few metres for prototypes to full deployment lengths

Indicative values. Each cable is specified for your project and confirmed in a drawing and data sheet.

How a defence and naval project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
