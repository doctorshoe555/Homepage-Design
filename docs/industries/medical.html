Medical cables

Medical devices need cables that are small, flexible, reliable and easy to clean. We build them for scanners, ventilators, medical lasers and surgical robots.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Imaging and scanner cables
  • Intensive-care ventilator cables
  • Medical laser and therapy equipment
  • Surgical robot cables

What you get

  • Miniaturised, very flexible constructions
  • Materials chosen for cleaning and skin contact requirements
  • Prototype quantities for device development
  • Stable series supply once your device is approved

How a medical project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
