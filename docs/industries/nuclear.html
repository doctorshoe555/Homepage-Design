Nuclear cables

Our radiation-resistant cables are installed in EPR reactors and in operating plants in France and abroad, as well as at reprocessing and research sites.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Power, control and command cables for moving bridges and handling equipment
  • Drag-chain cables for remote handling
  • Instrumentation cables in controlled areas
  • Composite cables with aramid reinforcement

What you get

  • Halogen-free and fire-retardant (C1/SH) constructions
  • Designs made to French nuclear specifications such as SGN
  • Experience on EPR projects in Finland, France and the UK
  • Documentation and traceability for each delivery
Design envelope
Typical ratingUp to 600 V
Temperature-20 °C to +80 °C (typical range)
Fire behaviourHalogen-free, C1/SH fire retardant
Reference sitesEPR projects, EDF 900 and 1300 MW plants, ORANO La Hague, CERN

Indicative values. Each cable is specified for your project and confirmed in a drawing and data sheet.

How a nuclear project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
