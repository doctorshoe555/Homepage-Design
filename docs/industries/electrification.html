Electrification cables

Port and airport electrification needs cables that are handled every day, dragged across quays and aprons, and still deliver safe power. That is the brief for STS-Flex and MULL'AIR 400.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Shore power and charging for all types of vessels
  • 400 Hz ground power for parked aircraft
  • Reeling and festoon systems
  • Cable sets with plugs for port and apron equipment

What you get

  • Helps ports and operators meet EU requirements for shore-side electricity
  • Flexible, abrasion-resistant sheaths for daily handling
  • Complete sets: cable, connectors and terminations from one supplier
  • Custom versions alongside the standard ranges

How a electrification project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
