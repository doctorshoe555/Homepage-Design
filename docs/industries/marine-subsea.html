Marine and subsea cables

A complete range of watertight cables for ROVs, underwater machines and offshore equipment, from neutrally buoyant tethers to armoured lift cables.

Request a design

Typical applications

  • Floating ROV tethers with optical fibres or coaxials
  • Main lift cables for ROVs
  • Umbilicals with power, video, command and hoses
  • Diving bell and diver umbilicals
  • Mooring line and rig mast cables
  • Seafarm, fishing and pool robot cables

What you get

  • Buoyancy, weight in water and breaking load tuned to your vehicle
  • Watertight constructions in special polyolefin compounds
  • Connectors, terminations and fittings supplied with the cable
  • Thousands of proven subsea designs to start from

How a marine and subsea project runs

The same six steps for every cable, adapted to the qualification your sector requires.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
