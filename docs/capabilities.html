Engineering, production and testing under one roof

We design the cable, make it, terminate it and prove it works. That is how we deliver one-off lengths quickly and series reliably.

Custom cable engineering

Our design office and R&D team work on electrical, pneumatic, optical, hydraulic and support cables. We combine them into hybrid constructions when one cable has to do the work of several.

  • Any length and any size, including short lengths
  • From miniaturised conductors to large cross-sections
  • Material selection for heat, cold, radiation, seawater, oil and abrasion
  • Drawings and data sheets for each design

Connectors, terminations and equipment

We deliver cables ready to install: fitted with connectors, terminations and fittings, on reels or in sets, so you receive a working system rather than a cable and a list of parts.

  • Connector assembly and overmoulding
  • Mechanical terminations and strain relief
  • Reels, drums and handling accessories

Testing and certification

Because every solution is custom, we bring in the certifying body your project requires and certify one-off products when needed. Electrical, mechanical and environmental tests are planned with you during the engineering study.

Our process

Six steps, one project team, and a clear decision point after each.

  1. Tell us the job

    Share your application, environment and constraints online or with our sales team.

  2. Engineering study

    Our design office proposes a construction, materials and a drawing.

  3. Prototype

    We produce a sample or short length so you can test it in real conditions.

  4. Qualification

    Tests and certification with the body your project requires.

  5. Production

    Manufacturing in Grimaud, in the length and quantity you need.

  6. Delivery and support

    Delivery with documentation, and support for repeat orders.
